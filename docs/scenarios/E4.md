# E4: Staff and a PA to board the evacuation train

**The drill.** An evacuation train waited at the platforms. A zone-specific
PA told platform passengers to board it and concourse passengers to use the
street exits, and two RCIs helped. 44 of the 61 people off the train
re-boarded. The concourse moved at 1:15 and was clear by about 4:00, the
escalator crowd moved at 1:30, and the station was clear at 6:45.

**The model** (`experiments/scenarios/E4.yaml`): 96 people (about 35 in the
station and 61 off the train).

- **Trains** at every platform from −0:05, waiting 10 minutes.
- **Alarm** at 0:00, cue weak. Escalators D, E and F close.
- **Zone-specific PA** at 0:05.
    - Platforms: "board the train immediately", cue strong, board_train.
    - Concourse: "leave via the nearest street exit", cue strong,
      leave_station.
- **General PA** at 0:25, cue medium, leave_station.
- **RCIs.**
    - Both go to the concourse, directing people as they go.
    - One holds the concourse (leave_station).
    - One patrols the platforms (board_train), all with cue strong.

**Targets and the fitted rule engine:**

| | Proulx | Rule-based |
|---|---:|---:|
| Concourse moves | 75 | 137 ± 6 |
| Escalator crowd moves | 90 | 117 ± 25 |
| Station clear | 405 | 517 ± 24 |

E4 is **held out**. Most people are out near Proulx's time; a slow tail
clears about 2 minutes late.

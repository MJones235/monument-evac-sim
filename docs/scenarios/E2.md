# E2: Staff direct the evacuation

**The drill.** Two Revenue Control Inspectors (RCIs) directed people on foot.
One held the concourse, and also made a PA announcement; the other cleared the
platforms. Passengers responded at once to the RCIs. The concourse crowd (30)
moved at 2:15, the escalator crowd (53) at 3:00. The concourse was clear by
about 3:00, the lower levels by 5:00, and the whole station at 8:00. The staff
could not cover every access point, and sent people through the fire-affected
route.

**The model** (`experiments/scenarios/E2.yaml`): 83 people.

- **Train** at Platform 1 from −0:05 (30 s dwell).
- **Alarm** at 0:00, cue weak. Escalators D, E and F close.
- **PA** at 0:25: "Please evacuate the station immediately via the nearest
  street exit", cue medium, leave_station.
- **RCIs.**
    - Both walk to the concourse from the alarm, directing people as they go
      (cue strong).
    - One then holds the concourse.
    - The other patrols Platforms 1 and 3, sending people up escalators B and C
      (cue strong, route B/C).

**Targets and the fitted rule engine:**

| | Proulx | Rule-based |
|---|---:|---:|

E2 is **held out**: the rule engine was not fitted to it. People start moving
about on time, but a tail of slow responders keeps the station open about 5
minutes too long.

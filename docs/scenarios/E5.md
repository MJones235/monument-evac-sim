# E5: Directive PA with the fire's location

**The drill.** No staff. A PA every 20 s named the suspected fire (the N/S
escalators), told platform passengers to board the first train and concourse
passengers to leave by the nearest exit. The crowd at the bottom of the N/S
escalators moved immediately on hearing "suspected fire" (1:00). The
concourse moved at 1:30 and was clear by about 3:00. The station was clear at
5:45, apart from two groups with prams at 7:00 and 10:15. E5 was the most
effective drill.

**The model** (`experiments/scenarios/E5.yaml`): 103 people (about 35 in the
station and 68 off the trains).

- **Trains** at every platform from −0:05, waiting 10 minutes.
- **Alarm** at 0:00, cue weak. Escalators D, E and F close.
- **Directive PA** from 0:05, every 20 s.
    - Platforms: cue strong, board_train.
    - Concourse: cue strong, leave_station.

**Targets and the fitted rule engine:**

| | Proulx | Rule-based |
|---|---:|---:|
| Concourse moves | 90 | 123 ± 6 |
| Escalator crowd moves | 60 | 103 ± 15 |
| Station clear | 345 | 366 ± 31 |

E5 is one of the drills the rule engine is fitted to; it fixes the
strong-cue delay.

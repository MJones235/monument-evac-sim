# E3: Minimal PA

**The drill.** A PA repeated "Please evacuate the station immediately" every
20 s, with no further information and no staff. The concourse crowd (13) moved
at 1:15. The crowd at the bottom of the N/S escalators (48) did not move until
the Fire Brigade reached them at 7:40, and the firemen there did not tell
them about another route. The station was clear at 10:30.

**The model** (`experiments/scenarios/E3.yaml`): 61 people.

- **Trains** at every platform from −0:05 (60 s dwell).
- **Alarm** at 0:00, cue weak. Escalators D, E and F close.
- **PA** from 0:05, every 20 s, cue medium, leave_station.
- **Fire Brigade** from 7:25 at the bottom of the N/S escalators, then the
  concourse, naming escalators B and C (cue strong).

**Targets and the fitted rule engine:**

| | Proulx | Rule-based |
|---|---:|---:|
| Concourse moves | 75 | 275 ± 10 |
| Escalator crowd moves | 460 | 255 ± 10 |
| Station clear | 630 | 590 ± 66 |

!!! warning "A known misfit"
    In the drill the two crowds heard the same PA but responded 6 minutes
    apart. In the model they get the same medium cue, so no delay fits both:
    the fit settles between them. Either the escalator crowd did not hear or
    trust the PA there, which would be a weaker cue for that zone, or this is
    a limit of a cue-strength model. That is a modelling decision still to be
    made.

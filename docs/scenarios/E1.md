# E1: Fire alarm only

**The drill.** The alarm sounded; nobody gave any other information. 93% of
passengers heard it, but 76% thought it was not a real emergency, and people
carried on as normal. The concourse crowd (66) started moving at 8:15, when
the Fire Brigade arrived and directed them. The crowd at the bottom of the N/S
escalators (45) moved at 9:00. The exercise was stopped at 14:47 with people
still inside. This is the control condition.

**The model** (`experiments/scenarios/E1.yaml`): 111 people.

- **Alarm** at 0:00, cue weak. Escalators D, E and F close.
- **Firefighters** at the N/S escalators from the alarm, cue weak.
- **Fire Brigade** from 8:00 on the concourse, then the bottom of the N/S
  escalators: "You must leave the station immediately", cue strong.

**Targets and the fitted rule engine** (3 seeds, seconds after the alarm):

| | Proulx | Rule-based |
|---|---:|---:|
| Concourse moves | 495 | 397 ± 6 |
| Escalator crowd moves | 540 | 567 ± 23 |
| Station clear | never | never (3/3) |

E1 is one of the drills the rule engine is fitted to; it fixes the weak-cue
delay.
